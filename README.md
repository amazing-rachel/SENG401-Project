**SENG 401 Team Project**

Our team was tasked to create a serious game focused on one of the United Nations 17 Sustainable Development Goals. We focused on SDG 4: Quality Education, which aims to ensure all children complete free primary and secondary schooling by 2030. 

We created an educational version of the classic Snakes and Ladders game, called:
**3D Snakes & Ladders: Education Edition**

- Choose from a variety of educational subjects. such as Math or Science. Roll the dice by answering a simple question, go up a ladder by answering an applied knowledge question, and stop yourself from going down a snake with a challenge question! Have fun!
- Our game targets a younger audience, aiming to engage its users in learning and refining pre-existing knowledge. This meaningful and accessible educational project integrates curriculum-aligned questions into a familiar board game format, ensuring that player progression is driven by learning rather than chance.
